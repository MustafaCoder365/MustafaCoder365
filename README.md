### 🦷 ClinLeen — SaaS platform for dental clinics

My own product: a multi-tenant platform where any dental clinic subscribes and runs its whole practice, across branches, in Arabic or English.

- **Interactive dental chart** (FDI), complete patient files, treatment plans, prescriptions, and consents
- **Appointments** with conflict prevention, a live waiting room, and a public booking page for patients
- **Billing** with installments and local payment methods, cash register, expenses, inventory, and dental labs
- Roles and granular permissions, audit log, strict per-clinic data isolation, installable PWA

`Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `NextAuth` `Zod` `Tailwind CSS`

### 🍢 Dijla Grill — Restaurant website with 3D food and online ordering

A playful Baghdadi charcoal-grill concept. Food modeled in code floats around the title, and a scroll scene builds a samoon sandwich layer by layer.

- **Online ordering**: photo menu with Arabic-aware search and filters, an order panel, and checkout straight to WhatsApp
- **Dish details** with options and pairings, a **QR table menu** page, **table booking**, and group deals
- **Real-time 3D** that only renders while visible, with lighter scenes on phones

`Next.js 16` `Three.js` `React Three Fiber` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS 4`

### 🦷 Lumina Dental — Dental clinic website

A dental clinic concept whose visual identity comes from Baghdad's kashi tilework.

- **"Where does it hurt?"**: an interactive mouth map (FDI) that turns a tooth and a symptom into a likely treatment and price

- Live "open now" status, validated booking for Iraqi numbers, WhatsApp follow-up
